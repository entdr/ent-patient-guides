Chronic sinusitis and FESS sinus surgery

Show Image

Is a blocked nose always sinusitis?

No. A deviated septum, swollen turbinates, allergic rhinitis and nasal polyps can all keep the nose blocked for months, and each is treated differently. Chronic rhinosinusitis means symptoms for 12 weeks or more, such as a blocked or running nose, facial pressure or a weaker sense of smell, along with changes seen on nasal endoscopy or a CT scan (EPOS 2020).

Can nasal sprays make it worse?

Decongestant sprays such as xylometazoline and oxymetazoline are meant for a few days at a time. Used for weeks or months, they can cause rebound blockage called rhinitis medicamentosa (Zucker et al.). Steroid nasal sprays are different, and they are safe for long-term use under an ENT's guidance.

Does Delhi's air pollution matter?

Yes. Delhi averaged 82.2 µg/m³ of PM2.5 in 2025, the highest of any capital city in IQAir's report (The Wire). In a US study of about 4 billion outpatient visits, each 1 µg/m³ rise in PM2.5 predicted a 10% higher rate of chronic sinusitis visits (Yang et al.).

How is it diagnosed?

Two tests answer most questions: a nasal endoscopy, done in the clinic in a few minutes, and a CT scan of the sinuses. Together they show whether the sinuses are blocked and whether surgery would help. Not everyone needs surgery.

What should I try before surgery?

Daily saline rinses and a steroid nasal spray, used properly for several weeks, plus treatment of any allergy (EPOS 2020). Antibiotics help in a bacterial flare-up, but repeated courses rarely fix a long-standing blockage.

What is FESS?

FESS (functional endoscopic sinus surgery) opens the blocked sinuses through the nostrils, using a thin camera called an endoscope. There is no cut or scar on the face, and the nose keeps its shape. Most patients go home the same day or the next morning. Read more on the FESS page at entdelhi.com.

When should I see an ENT urgently?

Go the same day for swelling or redness around the eye, double or blurred vision, a severe headache or forehead swelling, high fever with a stiff neck, or confusion. A blockage on one side only, with bleeding, crusting or a bad smell, also needs a prompt check.
